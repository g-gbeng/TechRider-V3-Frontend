How it works

Five steps, and you can follow each one from your phone.

  1. Request

    Pick your device, tick what's wrong and add photos. Your address fills in from your location.

  2. Choose a technician

    Compare verified technicians by rating, experience and reviews from other customers.

  3. Approve the price

    You get one clear price that includes pickup and return. Pay securely with Paystack only if you agree.

  4. We collect it

    A rider picks up your device and takes it straight to your technician.

  5. Fixed and returned

    Your device comes back to your door. Give the rider your handover code, then rate the repair.

Why it's safe to hand over your device

Checked technicians and riders

Everyone who repairs or carries your device sends their ID and is approved by TechRider first.

Handover codes

Your device is only marked delivered when you read your 4-digit code to the rider, so it can't go to the wrong person.

Pay only when you agree

No payment until you approve the quote. Payments go through Paystack; we never see your card.

Disputes and refunds

If something goes wrong, raise a dispute and our team steps in. Paid jobs that get cancelled are refunded.
