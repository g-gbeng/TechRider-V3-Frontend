1. What TechRider does

TechRider is a platform in Lagos, Nigeria that connects customers who need a device repaired with independent, verified technicians, and arranges for verified riders to collect the device and bring it back. Technicians and riders are independent service providers, not TechRider employees. TechRider manages payments, logistics, support and disputes.

3. Technicians and riders: approval

Technicians and riders must apply and be approved before they can take work. When you apply you must provide:

  • Technicians: a clear photo of your face, a government ID (national ID, driver's licence or international passport), your business registration or licence, your business address, specialization and experience, and a bank account for payouts.
  • Riders: a clear photo of your face, a valid driver's licence, a government ID (national ID or international passport), your vehicle type, brand and plate number, and a bank account for payouts.

Documents must be genuine, valid and your own. We decide whether to approve each application and will tell you the outcome by email. We may ask for more information, and may withdraw approval if details turn out to be false or out of date.

4. Requests, quotes and prices

  • A customer describes the device and fault, adds photos and chooses a technician.
  • The technician sends a quote, which TechRider reviews. The customer then sees one total: a repair price, plus a logistics fee for collecting and returning the device. The logistics fee depends on the customer's zone (mainland or island).
  • The customer pays nothing until they accept a quote. They can decline it, and the request closes at no charge.

5. Payments

  • Customers pay through Paystack when accepting a quote. Pickup is only arranged after payment is confirmed.
  • All payments must go through TechRider. Technicians and riders must not ask for, and customers must not make, payments or tips outside the platform for TechRider jobs. Payments made outside TechRider aren't covered by our support, refunds or dispute process, and arranging them may lead to suspension.
  • Prices are in Nigerian naira and include the logistics shown in the quote.

6. Cancellations and refunds

  • Customers can cancel a request at any time before paying.
  • After payment, contact support to cancel. Once a device has been collected or a repair has started, cancellation may not be possible, or may be partial, depending on the work done.
  • When TechRider cancels a paid job, we refund the customer to the original payment method through Paystack. Banks may take several working days to show a refund.
  • TechRider may cancel a job, for example if no suitable technician or rider is available, if a device can't be repaired, or if we suspect fraud.

7. Pickups, returns and handover codes

  • When a rider collects a device, the person handing it over enters the rider's 4-digit pickup code in their TechRider app. Don't hand over a device if the code doesn't match. After 5 wrong attempts the check locks until TechRider unlocks it.
  • When a device is delivered, the receiver reads their handover code to the rider in order to collect the device.
  • Customers should back up their data, remove SIM and memory cards, and remove any case or accessories that aren't part of the repair before pickup. TechRider and technicians aren't responsible for data loss.
  • Please be available at the address you gave. Repeated missed pickups or returns may lead to cancellation.
