TechRider Help Center

Frequently asked questions for Customers, Technicians, and Riders.

General

What is TechRider?

TechRider gets phones, laptops, tablets, smartwatches and desktops repaired without you leaving home. You request a repair, choose a verified technician, see one clear price, and a verified TechRider rider collects your device and brings it back once it's fixed. You can follow every step from your phone.

Where does TechRider work?

We currently serve Lagos, on both the mainland and the island. When you request a repair you pick your zone, and your address fills in from your location if you allow it.

What do technicians and riders need to sign up?

Technicians give their name, email, phone, business address, specialization, years of experience, a bank account for payouts, and upload:

  • A clear photo of your face (JPG, PNG or WebP)
  • A government ID: national ID, driver's licence or international passport
  • Your business registration or licence

Riders give their name, email, phone, home address, vehicle type, brand and plate number, a bank account for payouts, and upload:

  • A clear photo of your face (JPG, PNG or WebP)
  • Your driver's licence: a photo of the front of a valid licence
  • A government ID: national ID or international passport

IDs and licences can be a photo (JPG, PNG, WebP) or a PDF, up to 5 MB each. Make sure every detail is sharp and readable, or we may have to ask you again.

Your documents are stored securely and only TechRider admins can open them, to verify who you are.

How long does approval take?

Every technician and rider application is checked by our team before the account goes live. Until then you'll see an "awaiting approval" page when you log in. We email you as soon as a decision is made. If your application isn't approved, the email tells you why, and you can contact us to fix it.

How do I reach TechRider support?

Use the chat on your request, job or delivery: it's a private conversation between you and the TechRider team. You can also email techridersupport@gmail.com, message us on WhatsApp (+234 906 943 6272) or find us on Instagram @techrider.ng.

Something went wrong. How do I raise a complaint?

Open Communications from your dashboard menu, choose what the problem is about (a payment, a delay, a damaged device, a delivery and so on) and describe it, including the job code if there is one. Our team reviews and settles it, and you can follow its status on the same page. You can also send us suggestions there.

Why has my account been suspended?

We may suspend an account that breaks our Terms of Service, for example by arranging payments outside TechRider, giving false documents or mistreating other users. A suspended account can't use the platform. Contact support if you think it's a mistake.

Customer FAQs

How do I request a repair?

Create a free account, then tap Request Repair. Choose the device type, brand and model, tick what's wrong (or describe it), add up to 4 photos, pick your zone and confirm your address. Then choose a technician by their rating, experience, completed jobs and reviews from other customers.

How is my repair priced?

Your technician looks at your request and sends a quote, which TechRider checks before it reaches you. You then see one total: the repair price (which includes TechRider's service fee) plus a logistics fee for collecting and returning your device. You pay nothing until you accept the quote.

How much is pickup and return?

The logistics fee covers both trips (to the technician and back to you) and depends on your zone, mainland or island. It's shown in your quote, so you always know the full amount before paying.

How do I pay?

When you accept a quote you're taken to Paystack's secure checkout to pay by card, bank transfer or the other options Paystack offers. TechRider never sees or stores your card details. Please don't pay technicians or riders directly: payments made outside TechRider aren't covered.

Can I cancel my repair request?

Yes, any time before you pay: open the request and tap Cancel. If you decline a quote, the request simply closes and you're not charged. After you've paid, message support from the request. If we cancel a paid job, your payment is refunded to the original payment method through Paystack; your bank may take a few working days to show it.

How do I know the rider is really from TechRider?

When the rider arrives, your request page asks for the rider's 4-digit code. Ask the rider for the code shown in their TechRider app and enter it. Only hand over your device if it matches. After 5 wrong tries the check locks: don't hand over the device, and message support.

You'll also see the rider's name, vehicle and phone number once they accept the trip.

What is my handover code?

When your repaired device is on its way back, a handover code appears on your request. Read it out to the rider only once the device is in your hands. The rider enters it to finish the delivery, which marks your repair as completed.

How do I track my repair?

Your Active Requests page shows each stage: awaiting technician, quote under review, quote ready, paid and awaiting pickup, pickup in progress, repair in progress, repair done, returning to you, and completed. You also get notifications and emails for the important steps.

Can I talk to my technician directly?

Messages go through TechRider support on your request, so your phone number stays private and we can help if anything goes wrong. You can call your rider during a pickup or return to arrange the handover.

Can I rate my technician and rider?

Yes. Once a repair is completed you can give your technician 1 to 5 stars, rate the rider who returned your device, and leave a written review. Ratings help other customers choose.

Technician FAQs

What documents do I need to sign up as a technician?

Along with your details, specialization, years of experience and a bank account for payouts, upload:

  • A clear photo of your face (JPG, PNG or WebP)
  • A government ID: national ID, driver's licence or international passport
  • Your business registration or licence

IDs and licences can be a photo (JPG, PNG, WebP) or a PDF, up to 5 MB each. Make sure every detail is sharp and readable, or we may have to ask you again.

How do I get repair jobs?

Customers choose you from the list of verified technicians. New requests appear in Available Jobs, where you can see the device, the fault and the customer's photos. Send a quote to take the job, or decline it so the customer can choose someone else.

How does quoting work?

Enter your price for the repair. TechRider reviews it and sends the customer one total that includes our service fee and the logistics fee. You're paid the price you quoted. The rider only collects the device once the customer has paid.

How do the pickup and handover codes work for me?

When a rider brings you a device, read your handover code to the rider once the device is in your hands. When a rider comes to collect a repaired device, ask for the 4-digit code in their app and enter it on your job page before handing it over. After 5 wrong tries the check locks and support has to unlock it.

How do I update a job?

From Active Jobs, mark the repair as in progress and then done. Once it's done, TechRider assigns a rider to return the device to the customer.

How and when do I get paid?

You earn your quoted price for every completed job. Once the device is back with the customer, TechRider pays you by bank transfer to the account you gave at signup. For questions about a payout or to change your bank details, contact support.

Can I contact customers directly?

No. Talk to TechRider support through the chat on the job and we'll pass on anything the customer needs to know. Please never ask customers to pay you outside TechRider: it can lead to suspension.

Rider FAQs

What documents do I need to sign up as a rider?

Along with your details, vehicle type, brand and plate number and a bank account for payouts, upload:

  • A clear photo of your face (JPG, PNG or WebP)
  • Your driver's licence: a photo of the front of a valid licence
  • A government ID: national ID or international passport

IDs and licences can be a photo (JPG, PNG, WebP) or a PDF, up to 5 MB each. Make sure every detail is sharp and readable, or we may have to ask you again.

How do I get deliveries?

TechRider assigns pickup and return trips to you. You get a notification and an email, then accept or decline the trip in Available Deliveries. You can have one active delivery at a time, and you'll see the addresses and phone numbers you need for it.

How does the pickup code work?

When you arrive to collect a device, show your 4-digit pickup code from your active delivery to the person handing it over. They enter it in their TechRider app, and the trip moves to Picked up by itself. If they enter a wrong code 5 times the pickup locks; call TechRider to unlock it.

How do I complete a delivery?

At the drop-off, hand over the device, ask the receiver for their handover code and enter it in your app. The code only works for that delivery, so it confirms the right person has the device.

How and when do I get paid?

You earn the trip fee for every delivery you complete. TechRider pays you by bank transfer to the account you gave at signup once the delivery is done. Each trip shows what you'll earn for it, and your History shows what you've earned. To change your bank details, contact support.

What if something goes wrong on a trip?

Message TechRider support from the delivery straight away, or call us on +234 906 943 6272. If the trip can't go ahead, our team can cancel or reassign it.

Can I see my delivery history?

Yes. Your History page lists your completed and cancelled deliveries.
