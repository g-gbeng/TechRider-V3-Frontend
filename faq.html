TechRider Help Center

Frequently asked questions for Customers, Technicians, and Riders.

Customer FAQs

How do I request a repair?

Go to the repair request page, choose your device category, describe the issue, upload images if needed, and select a technician.

Can I cancel my repair request?

Yes. Pending repair requests can be cancelled before a technician accepts them.

How do I track my repair progress?

You can monitor repair updates through your repair history and notifications pages in real time.

Technician FAQs

How do I accept a repair request?

Open your available jobs page and click Accept on any incoming repair request.

Can I reject a repair?

Yes. Rejected jobs are moved into the rejected requests section in admin monitoring.

How are technician earnings calculated?

Earnings are based on successfully completed repair jobs and approved payouts.

Rider FAQs

How do I receive delivery assignments?

Admins assign deliveries through the communications module and you receive notifications instantly.

What happens if delivery fails?

Failed deliveries must include a reason which is logged into the admin reports system.

Can I see my delivery history?

Yes. Your completed and failed deliveries are stored in your records page.
