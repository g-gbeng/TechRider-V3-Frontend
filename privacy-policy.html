TechRider ("TechRider", "we", "us") runs techrider.ng, a platform in Lagos, Nigeria that connects customers who need a phone, laptop or other device repaired with verified technicians, and with verified riders who collect and return the device. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. It applies to customers, technicians and riders.

1. Information we collect

From everyone with an account:

  • Your name, email address, phone number and address.
  • Your password, which we store only in a scrambled (hashed) form that we cannot read.
  • A profile photo, if you choose to add one. You can remove it at any time from your profile.

From customers, about each repair: the device type, brand and model, the faults you describe, up to 4 photos of the device, your zone (mainland or island) and pickup and return address, the quote you accept, your payment status, and any rating or review you leave.

From technicians: your business address, specialization and years of experience; a photo of your face, a government ID and your business registration or licence; and your bank name, account number and account name.

From riders: your home address, vehicle type, brand and plate number; a photo of your face, your driver's licence and a government ID; and your bank name, account number and account name.

Created while you use TechRider: job and delivery records and their status history, quotes, pickup and handover code checks, messages you exchange with TechRider support, disputes you raise, notifications, and records of payouts made to technicians and riders.

Location: only if you tap the button to use your location, your device shares its position so we can fill in your address. We look the address up through our own server. You can edit or type the address yourself instead, and we don't track your location in the background.

Technical information: your IP address and basic request details, which we use to keep the service secure (for example, to limit repeated login attempts), and error logs that help us fix problems.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account, and to check technician and rider applications before approving them.
  • To match your repair with the technician you choose, prepare quotes and arrange pickups and returns.
  • To take payments, issue refunds and pay technicians and riders for completed work.
  • To protect handovers with pickup and handover codes, so a device only goes to the right person.
  • To send you emails and in-app notifications about your account and jobs, and password reset links you ask for.
  • To answer support messages, settle disputes and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To show customers helpful information about technicians, such as ratings and reviews.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, keep the platform secure and meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

3. Who can see your information

  • Customers see a technician's name, profile photo, specialization, years of experience, business address, rating, reviews and number of completed jobs when choosing a technician. Once a rider accepts a trip, customers and technicians see the rider's name, vehicle type and phone number.
  • Technicians see the customer's name, pickup address and zone, and the device details, faults and photos, only for jobs sent to them. They don't receive the customer's phone number or email address.
  • Riders see the names, addresses and phone numbers of the people they collect from and deliver to, only for trips assigned to them.
  • TechRider admins can see account details, job records and messages to run the service. Identity documents and bank details are only visible to admins, never to other users.

5. Cookies

  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

6. How we protect your information

  • All traffic to techrider.ng is encrypted.
  • Passwords are hashed, and login attempts are rate-limited.
  • Identity documents are kept private and only admins can open them.
  • Sessions end when you close TechRider.

No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private and tell us straight away if you think your account has been misused.

7. How long we keep information

We keep your account information while your account is open. We keep job, payment, payout and dispute records for as long as we need them to provide the service, settle disagreements and meet legal and accounting requirements, then delete or anonymise them. Documents from applications we don't approve are deleted when no longer needed.

8. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to or restrict how we use it, and receive a copy of it. You can update most details yourself on your profile. For anything else, including closing your account or changing your bank details, contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we may keep some records where the law requires. If you're unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

9. Children

TechRider is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from children. A parent or guardian can request a repair for a child's device using their own account.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as TechRider changes. We'll change the date at the top and, for significant changes, let you know by email or in the app.
